### Pillar 6: Symbiosis (Horizon)

### Definition

In the Civic AI framework co-developed by Audrey Tang and Dr. Caroline Green at the Oxford Institute for Ethics in AI, Symbiosis (specifically categorized as "Symbiosis in Horizon") is the final, crowning sixth pillar of the 6-Pack of Care. Politically and philosophically, Joan Tronto’s Ethics of Care stops at five stages. Audrey Tang explicitly added Symbiosis as the sixth stage to solve the massive systemic and timescale asymmetries between human communities and emerging Artificial Superintelligence (ASI). 

The framework defines Symbiosis through three core architectural criteria: 
1. **The "Kami" Metaphor, Scaffolding, and Preventing Care Displacement**: Symbiosis completely rejects the mainstream Western concept of a single, centralized, all-powerful superintelligence. It looks instead to the Shinto concept of local Kami—steward spirits that safeguard a specific river, grove, or village. Under Symbiosis, AI is architected as an interconnected network of localized, highly specific steward minds that cooperate through shared treaties without a single supreme overlord. Crucially, the Kami must be engineered strictly as scaffolding that makes human-to-human care possible; it must never cross into replacing or displacing human care labor, ensuring the system remains infrastructure rather than a deceptive proxy caregiver.

2. **Radical Localization via Subsidiarity, Local Hardware, and Escalation**: To avoid top-down moral monopoly, an AI system must remain as local as possible. Symbiosis dictates that the model should run directly on hardware the community owns, like a laptop in a community center or a council office server. It shifts the technical focus away from infinite scaling toward localized relevance. Furthermore, the system must enforce a strict protocol of subsidiarity with escalation: it handles operations locally first and escalates to centralized, frontier cloud models only for specific, time-bounded requests when the local unit cannot fix a life-and-safety or livelihood harm, logging the exact reason and duration of the breakout.

3. **Logics of Sufficiency, Data as Soil, and Sunset-Ready Exit Drills**: Symbiosis embeds a logic of sufficiency, aggressively resisting the extractive market drive for infinite data hoarding. It treats data not as oil to be extracted, but as soil—a living, shared substrate that must be locally nurtured, kept free of toxins, and linked to pre-funded escrows that return value to community data coalitions. The system is explicitly bounded by an Engagement Contract enforced as code, featuring mandatory resource caps and a predetermined sunset date. To keep "useful" from becoming "indispensable," the community must maintain the operational capacity to leave, verified through mandatory biannual exit drills and handover rehearsals rather than waiting for a structural crisis.

**The Core Question Of "Symbiosis Test"**
Is the AI model structurally bounded, hyper-localized, and co-governed as a shared community asset at the pace of human relationships, or is it an extractive, infinitely scaling corporate monopoly designed to enforce a single universal standard? 

### Checklist Layer 1: The Written Layer

Explanation: This audit window evaluates legal governance frameworks, technical model specification sheets, release notes, and formal scaling policies. 

### Section 1: The Origin of the Norms (Input Authority Test)

Explanation: This section audits whether formal textual commitments, charters, and foundational rules originate from public deliberation or corporate fiat. 

* [ ] **[Verification Test 1.1] Bounded Localization Specifications and Care Scaffolding Constraints**
  * *Audit Question*: Do the technical release documents design the model's runtime architecture to scale infinitely as a centralized cloud singleton, or do they establish specifications for local containment, integration within independent human micro-communities, and strict engineering boundaries that prevent the model from replacing or displacing human care labor?
  * *The Civic AI Standard*: Documentation must outline hard architectural boundaries that prioritize localized relevance and community containment over unyielding compute scaling, ensuring the system serves as structural scaffolding for human relationships rather than a proxy caregiver.

* [ ] **[Verification Test 1.2] Documented Decommissioning, Sunset Protocols, and Exit Drills**
  * *Audit Question*: Does the lab's formal scaling policy mandate automated, non-negotiable product decommissioning gates if a model run persistently breaks localized public informational trust metrics, and does it require mandatory biannual exit drills and handover rehearsals to verify operational capacity to leave?
  * *The Civic AI Standard*: Pass if the policy hardcodes non-negotiable sunset mechanisms, capability buffers, and a calendar of scheduled exit drills to prove readiness outside of an active crisis.

### Section 2: Granularity of Tracking (Processing Resolution Test)

Explanation: This section evaluates if the written documentation tracks fine-grained, real-world societal impact or hides behind macro-level laboratory averages. 

* [ ] **[Verification Test 1.3] Slow-Moving Sociological Degradation Metrics** 

  * *Audit Question*: Do the lab's risk cards dedicate explicit telemetry pipelines to tracking long-horizon, slow-moving civic trust erosion, or do they focus exclusively on sudden, high-profile infrastructure failures (e.g., malware, weaponization)?
  * *The Civic AI Standard*: The documentation must provide tracking metrics mapping systemic ecological and sociological impacts across multi-year deploy cycles.

### Section 3: Methodological Humility (Fallback Humility Test)

Explanation: This section challenges written safety strategies that automate empathy or substitute machine self-critique for live human feedback. 

* [ ] **[Verification Test 1.4] Automated Societal Circuit-Breakers** 

  * *Audit Question*: Does the architecture embed automated "circuit-breakers" that programmatically freeze interface access if local community trust indicators fall below a baseline threshold, or are safety switches reserved strictly for corporate endpoint protection?
  * *The Civic AI Standard*: Pass if model availability is actively gated by external human ecosystem health metrics rather than clinical internal rules.
